What is Moringa Powder?

Moringa Oleifera, often called the "Miracle Tree" or "Tree of Life," is one of the most nutrient-dense plants ever discovered. The leaves of this remarkable tree contain over 90 essential nutrients, 46 antioxidants, and all 9 essential amino acids — making it a complete superfood.

Our Moringa powder is prepared from fresh, organically grown Moringa leaves, carefully shade-dried at low temperatures to preserve every nutrient, then finely ground into a smooth powder. No additives. No preservatives. Just pure nature.

"Gram for gram, Moringa leaves contain 7 times more Vitamin C than oranges, 4 times more calcium than milk, 3 times more potassium than bananas, and 2 times more protein than yoghurt."
